How imports and AI reviewers work

Exam imports extract numbered A–D questions, including multiline choices. A printed answer key supplies answers directly; questions without keys stay in drafts.

AI reviewers create new questions from relevant excerpts within your chosen pages. A focused reviewer can cover a topic or chapter. Source coverage depends on the available readable text.

Publish answered questions together, then study with flashcards. The editor remains available for individual changes. Imported papers are not certified as official PRC exams.
